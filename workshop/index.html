Get started.

Install the Held in Common app, make your node on your own computer, add your profile, and share a signed copy to the workshop commons. Your originals stay on your node.

1 · Download the app
macOS
Apple Silicon

All Linux downloads →
2 · Set up
Install and open it

Mac: open the .dmg, drag Held In Common into Applications, and open it from there. No Terminal needed.

Linux: install the .deb or .rpm, or make the AppImage executable and run it (install libfuse2 if it won’t start).

Choose “A node” and name it

The app asks what you want to run: choose A node, your own commons on your own computer. Give it a name, for example “Studio laptop”. People see it next to your profile.

Add your profile, and keep your recovery code

Click Add your profile, pick a handle (your @name) and a passphrase. A forgotten passphrase can’t be recovered.

Your recovery code is shown once. Write it down and keep it offline: it takes your handle back if your keys are ever stolen.

Then fill in your bio and photo, choose who can see it and a licence, and Publish. It’s saved on your computer only.

Share to the workshop commons

On your profile, under Share to a commons, open Share with an invite code, or to another commons, enter the workshop address and press Share:

https://heldincommon.cerberus-broadnose.ts.net

Got an invite code? Paste it in and you’re listed straight away. Without one you’ll see Waiting for a vouch until a member vouches for you or the steward approves you. Press Check now to see if you’re in.

You’re in the commons.

Your originals stay on your node. Members-only work never leaves it. You can withdraw what you shared at any time.
