Get started.

Install the Held in Common app, make your node on your own computer, add your profile, and share a signed copy to the workshop commons. Your originals stay on your node.

2 · Set up
Install and open it

Mac: open the .dmg, drag Held In Common into Applications, and open it from there. No Terminal needed.

Linux: install the .deb or .rpm, or make the AppImage executable and run it (install libfuse2 if it won’t start).

Choose “A node” and name it

The app asks what you want to run: choose A node, your own commons on your own computer. Give it a name, for example “Studio laptop”. People see it next to your profile. You can rename it later from This computer, the panel in the sidebar.

Add your profile, and keep your recovery code

Click Add your profile, pick a handle (your @name) and a passphrase. A forgotten passphrase can’t be recovered.

Your recovery code is shown once. Write it down and keep it offline: it takes your handle back if your keys are ever stolen.

Then fill in your bio and photo, choose who can see it, a licence and signals, and Publish. It’s saved on your computer only.

Share to the workshop commons

Once your profile is published, your node offers the next step: press Share to Held in Common Collective Commons. A signed copy goes to the commons; the original stays on your computer.

To share somewhere else, or with an invite code, open Share with an invite code, or to another commons under Share to a commons, enter the address and press Share. The workshop commons is at:

https://heldincommon.cerberus-broadnose.ts.net

Got an invite code? Paste it in and you’re listed straight away. Without one you’ll see Waiting for a vouch until a member vouches for you or the steward approves you. Press Check now to see if you’re in.

In a room session? Paste the link the host shows for their local commons (it ends in /join/ and a code). Someone’s node opened to the room shows their profile but can’t take yours.

Hosting a session? Under This computer in the sidebar, choose Host this room…. It starts a local commons, opens it to everyone on your Wi-Fi and shows a QR code and join link to put on the screen. Your node stays as it is.

Write a story

Go to Stories → Write a story. The workshop prompt: Listen to your street for a minute. Write a small story from what you hear. It stays on your computer until you press Share, and you can withdraw it from the commons at any time.

On the rights step you can write your own terms for AI. If you like, tick Share these terms with the Held in Common project for research: we receive your terms only, never your name, handle or story.

You’re in the commons.

Your originals stay on your node. Members-only work never leaves it. You can withdraw what you shared at any time.
